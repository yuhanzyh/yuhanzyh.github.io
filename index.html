Academic homepage

Intelligent, safe, and sustainable mobility.

I develop intelligent methods for safer automated driving and more sustainable urban mobility.

My research connects large language models, traffic simulation, driving behavior, and data-driven optimization. I am particularly interested in building transportation systems that are safer, more efficient, and easier to test and reproduce.

Research Interests

  • Autonomous Driving Testing. Scenario generation, simulation-based validation, driving behavior, and safety evaluation for connected and automated vehicles.
  • Large Language Models for Transportation. LLM-based structured traffic-scene generation, intelligent transportation applications, and human-vehicle-environment interaction.
  • Sustainable Urban Mobility. Shared-mobility operations, energy-efficiency modelling, scheduling optimization, and low-altitude infrastructure planning.
